# Portofino Editorial Shot List

One luxury magazine editorial, photographed over a single vacation. Every moment gets two purpose-built frames — a **Hero** (destination storytelling) and an **Editorial Detail** (outfit storytelling) — captured within the same scene but never duplicating each other.

---

## Global Photography Standard (applies to every frame)

- **Muse:** Mediterranean muse (Lilla) — identity-locked across the entire Portofino collection. Same face, hair color, and body across all 24 frames.
- **Grade:** warm golden-hour, soft Kodak-Portra-like film tones, gentle highlight roll-off, no cool/teal shadows.
- **Skin:** sun-kissed Mediterranean glow, natural pores visible, never plastic-smooth.
- **White balance:** warm neutral (~5200K), consistent across day and evening frames.
- **Contrast:** medium, filmic. No HDR clarity, no crushed blacks.
- **Lens:** 35mm full-frame equivalent for heroes, 50–85mm for editorial details. No fisheye, no ultra-wide distortion.
- **Subject scale:** Lilla occupies ~60–65% of frame height in heroes; editorial details may go tighter (¾-length or waist-up).
- **Headroom:** ~15–20% above the head on heroes so page titles / hero overlays sit cleanly in negative space.
- **Posing:** authentic candid editorial — natural body language, mid-motion, never runway-static.
- **Not-allowed:** studio lighting, direct-to-camera runway posing, "campaign" symmetry, flat overcast light, luxury-catalog blankness.

### Hero vs. Editorial Detail — the difference

The two frames must **read as two photographs from the same shoot**, not two crops of the same pose. Vary at minimum:

- camera distance
- body angle relative to camera
- facial direction / gaze
- hand placement
- moment in time (walking phase, seated shift, mid-sip, mid-turn)

## 1. Arrival Day

**Story:** the first breath of Portofino — stepping off the transfer, pastel façades revealing themselves.

### Hero
- **Tone:** anticipation, quiet awe.
- **Distance / lens:** wide, 35mm, full-length.
- **Angle:** ¾ from behind, slight low angle so the harbor rises around her.
- **Body:** mid-stride toward the piazzetta, one hand loosely holding a small weekender.
- **Gaze:** toward the pastel buildings, not to camera.
- **Environment:** cobblestones, painted façades, boats behind — architecture does 60% of the storytelling.
- **Composition:** Lilla left third, harbor opening right.
- **Wardrobe:** arrival tailoring — linen set or trench-over-slip, subtle.
- **Accessories:** weekender bag, sunglasses on head.
- **Movement:** walking, slight fabric motion.

### Editorial Detail
- **Distance / lens:** ¾-length, 50mm.
- **Angle:** front-¾ from lower right; she has paused.
- **Body:** stopped beside a painted wall, weight on one leg, weekender at her feet.
- **Gaze:** down at her sunglasses in-hand.
- **Environment:** tight against a coral/ochre plaster wall — architecture as texture, not story.
- **Wardrobe emphasis:** the tailoring's drape, the fabric, the shoe.
- **Differs from hero:** paused vs. walking, front vs. rear, wall vs. harbor, bag down vs. carried.

## Production notes

- Shoot / generate the two frames per moment **as a pair**, in the same session — never in isolation weeks apart. That's what preserves the "one editorial" feeling.
- Keep a **reference contact sheet** of all 24 approved frames pinned in the founder review workflow. Any new frame gets compared against the sheet for grade + skin tone before it replaces a live asset.
- When a frame is regenerated, always regenerate its companion in the same batch so the pair stays coherent.
- Locked assets (Pool Lounging hero video, Pool Lounging Splendido editorial detail) are the current grade + skin-tone reference — all other moments should match them.
