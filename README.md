# Resort Edit Magazine

Create a luxury editorial travel-fashion affiliate website for my brand “Resort Edit.”

The aesthetic should feel like a high-end Mediterranean fashion magazine inspired by Vogue resort editorials, Slim Aarons vacation photography, luxury travel campaigns, and brands like Melissa Odabash, Pucci, Zimmermann, Johanna Ortiz, and Dolce & Gabbana.

The overall feeling should be:
editorial, aspirational, sophisticated, feminine, worldly, elegant, and luxurious coastal Mediterranean glamour.

TARGET AUDIENCE:
Stylish women in their late 30s and 40s who value luxury travel, elevated fashion, beautiful destinations, and sophisticated experiences.

IMPORTANT STYLE DIRECTION:
The fashion should feel sexy, polished, feminine, and sophisticated without showing too much skin.

Avoid:

overly young influencer styling

fast-fashion aesthetics

loud TikTok fashion energy

Instead use:

flowing maxis

tasteful slits

polished vacation styling

The mood should feel like:
“a sophisticated worldly woman vacationing on the Italian Riviera.”

IMPORTANT FASHION POSITIONING:

The styling should feature a mix of:

luxury investment pieces

designer-inspired resortwear

The website should feel aspirational and sophisticated while still offering wearable, realistic shopping options for stylish women in their 30s and 40s.

Avoid overt “dupe culture” language or discount-fashion aesthetics. The tone should feel polished, elevated, and editorial rather than bargain-focused.
